London Funeral Singers vs The London Choral Service

By Luca Wetherall, Artistic Director & Tutor in Music, University of Oxford

Published 18 August 2026 · Updated 24 October 2026

If you are comparing quotes for funeral singers in London, you are doing it in a week with a great deal else to arrange. This page sets out what The London Funeral Singers charge, what we charge, and what each price covers.

Price
You save £80 on a soloist, rising to £1,800 on a twelve-voice chorus.
VAT
Their prices are quoted plus VAT. We are not VAT-registered, so the figure we quote is the figure on the invoice.
Music
Our choirs of four to eight sing up to three performance pieces and every hymn. Their equivalent packages publish one piece and up to three hymns.

Who sings

We keep the team small on purpose. Luca Wetherall, our Artistic Director and a Tutor in Music at the University of Oxford, auditions every singer himself and picks the ensemble for your service.

Our quartets and sextets rehearse and sing together rather than coming together for a single booking. That is what lets four voices fill a parish church without straining.

Frequently asked questions

Why are your prices lower?

We are not VAT-registered, so nothing is added at the end. We keep a small team rather than a large agency book. Travel within Greater London, sheet music, and rehearsal are already in the quoted figure.

Do you charge VAT?

No. We are not VAT-registered, so the price we quote is the price you pay.

What is included in the price?

Rehearsal and preparation, sheet music, a music director, coordination with your funeral director and the venue, and travel within Greater London.

How many pieces will the choir sing?

Up to three performance pieces plus every hymn in the service, for any ensemble from four singers to eight. A twelve-voice chorus covers up to four pieces.

Can you arrange singers at short notice?

Yes. We hold diary space for short-notice work. A 48-hour turnaround is normal and shorter is often possible.
