Hear our singers and musicians

A selection of the pieces our clients request most often, performed by our singers and instrumentalists.

Most requested

Abide With Me — Full Choir

One of the most beloved funeral hymns in the English tradition. The choral harmonies bring a depth of consolation and warmth that is deeply moving in a service setting.

He Shall Feed His Flock — Soloist, from Handel’s Messiah

One of the most tender and consoling arias in all of Handel's Messiah. Gentle, unhurried, and profoundly peaceful — a beautiful choice for a funeral or memorial service.

Be Thou My Vision — Full Choir

An ancient Irish hymn with a timeless, haunting melody. Especially beautiful in a choral setting with organ accompaniment.

Ave Maria — Soloist

Schubert's beloved setting — by far the most requested piece across all our services. Luminous, moving, and profoundly comforting. Traditionally performed by a solo soprano.

How Great Thou Art — Full Choir

A hymn of tremendous power. The choral harmonies build gradually, culminating in one of the most stirring, unforgettable climaxes in all congregational music. Genuinely transcendent.

Ave Verum Corpus — Full Choir

Mozart's sublime motet — just four minutes of extraordinary beauty, serenity, and grace. One of the perfect pieces of choral music ever written.

The Lord's My Shepherd — Small Choir

The most beloved hymn at funerals across the United Kingdom, sung here to the well-known Crimond melody. Gentle, familiar, and deeply comforting.

Amazing Grace — Soloist

Perhaps the world's best-known hymn. As powerful a solo as in a full choral arrangement.

Jerusalem — Full Choir

Parry's magnificent setting of Blake's visionary poem. A favourite at weddings, memorials, and national occasions — thrilling with a full choir and organ.

Something different

Fields of Gold — Soloist

Sting's gentle, luminous ballad. Perfectly suited to a solo voice with piano accompaniment — reflective and warm, without being sad. A beautiful choice for celebrations of life.

Somewhere Over the Rainbow — Soloist

A timeless classic that speaks to hope and yearning without demanding sadness. Disarmingly simple, extraordinarily affecting. Often chosen to capture someone's optimism or adventurous spirit.

In Paradisum — Small Choir

The closing movement of the Fauré Requiem — ethereal, weightless, and profoundly tender. One of the most beautiful pieces in the choral repertoire.

Danny Boy — Soloist

The beloved Irish air, set to the Londonderry melody. Performed with warmth and feeling, it never fails to move.

Time to Say Goodbye — Soloist

Dramatic, soaring, and unmistakable. A bold choice that makes a powerful statement at celebrations of life.

Hymns

Make Me a Channel of Your Peace — Small Choir

The prayer of St Francis, set to a gentle, singable melody that congregations find easy to join in with.

Guide Me, O Thou Great Redeemer — Full Choir

The great Welsh hymn, sung to Cwm Rhondda. Rousing, powerful, and guaranteed to lift the spirits of everyone present.

All Things Bright and Beautiful — Small Choir

A joyful, uplifting hymn that celebrates the natural world. Particularly well suited to celebrations of life and outdoor services.

This is just a sample of what we offer. If there’s a piece you’ve always imagined hearing at your occasion — classical, sacred, contemporary, or something completely different — we can perform virtually anything, and we’re happy to create bespoke arrangements for your occasion.

For help choosing the right pieces, explore our funeral music guide or wedding hymns guide.
