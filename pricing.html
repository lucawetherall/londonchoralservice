Choir and singer pricing

Last updated: May 2026

Clear, transparent pricing. What you see is what you pay — no hidden fees, no surprises. Prices include all fees and taxes. Travel costs may apply for venues beyond Greater London.

Choirs & singers

Ensemble Details Price
Soloist One trained singer performs up to three pieces and leads the congregation through all the hymns. In smaller chapels and crematoriums, a soloist sits close to the guests, and people often remark on how personal it feels. We match the singer’s voice type to your chosen repertoire. Most families spend more on flowers than on a singer who carries the entire service. See our funeral music guide or wedding music guide for help choosing pieces. From £215
Small Choir 4 singers Four singers in soprano, alto, tenor, and bass give you full harmony on hymns and up to three performance pieces. They fill a church seating 50 to 200 without overpowering the room. Guests who have never sung a hymn in their lives find themselves joining in. The four-part sound is what most people picture when they think of a choir at a ceremony. From £1,150
Quintet 5 singers A second soprano rounds out the sound and opens up repertoire that a four-part group cannot cover. You hear stronger support on the melody line, and the congregation sings with more confidence. Up to three performance pieces plus all hymns. The additional singer costs around £250 more than a quartet and opens up repertoire the quartet cannot cover. From £1,400
Sextet 6 singers Six singers produce a far bigger sound than a quartet at a fraction of a full choir’s cost. Up to three performance pieces plus all hymns. A sextet fills a crematorium or mid-sized church with ease. From £1,600
Full Choir 8 singers Eight singers suit large churches and cathedrals where sound needs to reach the back rows. The choir can split into eight-part harmony for pieces like Stanford or Parry. Up to three performance pieces plus all hymns. Eight voices in a cathedral acoustic produce a sound people describe years later. From £2,000
Chorus 12 singers Twelve singers fill even the largest cathedrals and concert halls. They handle complex works that smaller groups cannot attempt, from Fauré’s Requiem excerpts to large-scale anthem arrangements. Up to four performance pieces plus all hymns. From £3,000

Two or three singers are available with an organist or pianist: £215 for each singer and £215 for the accompanist, so £645 for two voices and organ, £860 for three. Unaccompanied groups start at four. Two or three voices cannot hold four-part harmony on their own, and in a church with no instrument the sound thins out where it matters most — under the melody.

Accompaniment & instruments

Service Details Price
Organist / Pianist Your organist or pianist plays hymn accompaniment and up to three performance pieces. If the venue has a pipe organ, we send an organist experienced with that instrument. For venues without one, we can bring a portable keyboard (additional costs may apply). Priced the same as a solo vocalist. From £215
Instrumentalists Pianists, harpists, strings A soloist, or an ensemble of four and above, chosen to suit your venue and programme. A harpist works well for arrivals music at weddings; a string quartet adds warmth to a memorial. We handle all the booking and coordination. Instrument hire and transport are included in the quoted price. From £215

Christmas & carol singers

Christmas bookings are charged at the table rates above, and carol bookings start with a Small Choir of four, since the singing is normally unaccompanied. A soloist is available for a sung grace or a single set piece at a dinner. A carol service in an office, livery hall, or hired church, or carol singers roaming a party or singing sets in a lobby, costs the same as the equivalent ensemble at any other time of year. Prices include all taxes, fees, and travel within Greater London.

A standard Christmas booking covers up to an hour and a half including breaks, or the length of a church service. The choir sings entirely a cappella where there is no instrument, and we can also provide an organist or pianist, or background piano between sets. Bringing a keyboard and speaker to a venue without a piano carries an extra charge, quoted upfront. Christmas Eve and Christmas Day dates carry a premium of around 25%, and December diaries usually close by mid-September. Our carol singers cost guide breaks down the options.

What happens next

Tell us about your occasion and we’ll send you a clear, written quote tailored to your venue and repertoire. If you’d like to go ahead, we take care of everything from that point: rehearsals, sheet music, and full coordination with your venue or funeral director. You won’t need to chase us or manage logistics. Most funeral bookings come through at short notice. Contact us and we will confirm availability the same day. If you are still deciding on repertoire, our celebration of life music and funeral songs guides are good starting points.

For funerals and weddings in London, there are no travel costs.

Your final quote may differ slightly depending on repertoire, venue requirements, or travel distance — we always confirm the price before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a funeral singer cost?

A solo funeral singer starts from £215. A small choir of four singers costs from £1,150, a quintet from £1,400, a sextet from £1,600, a full choir of eight from £2,000, and a twelve-voice chorus from £3,000. All prices include fees and taxes. We quote the same rates for funerals, weddings, memorials, and other ceremonies. Our popular funeral hymns guide can help you decide what music to include.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The price we quote is the price you pay. All fees and taxes are included. Travel costs may apply for venues outside Greater London, but we discuss those upfront before you commit. You will never receive an unexpected invoice.

Do prices include travel?

Prices shown cover venues within Greater London. For venues further afield, travel costs depend on the location and the number of singers. We calculate the travel supplement before you book and include it in your written quote, so you know the full cost from the start.

Can I get a custom quote?

Yes. Get in touch with the date, venue, and any details about the occasion. We will send a written quote within 24 hours. There is no obligation and no charge for the quote itself.

Can you provide music at short notice?

Yes. We regularly arrange funeral music at short notice, sometimes within 48 hours. Contact us with your date and we will confirm availability the same day. For weddings and other planned events, we recommend booking at least six months ahead.

Do your prices go up at Christmas?

No. December bookings are charged at the same rates as the rest of the year, inclusive of taxes, fees, and travel within Greater London. The exceptions are Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, which carry a premium of around 25%, confirmed in your written quote before you book.

How long does a Christmas carol booking last?

A standard Christmas booking covers up to an hour and a half including breaks, or the length of a church carol service. Longer programmes are possible for a premium fee, quoted upfront. Between sets we can provide background piano if your venue has an instrument.
