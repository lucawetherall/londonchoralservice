What a choir of four adds

Four singers (soprano, alto, tenor and bass) sing every hymn in full harmony and lead the congregation, so the room sings with confidence even when few people know the words. They also sing up to three pieces while everyone listens. Four voices fill a church seating 50 to 200.

In a small chapel or crematorium, one soloist can lead the hymns and sing up to three pieces.
