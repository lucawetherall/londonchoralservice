Music for your wedding

Professional singers for your ceremony, with the music planned with you by our Artistic Director, Luca Wetherall. Below are the moments a choir usually sings and the pieces couples ask for most. If a song means something to you, send it to us and we will arrange it for your singers.

When the singers sing

  • Arrivalwhile guests take their seats
  • Processionalas you walk in
  • Hymnsled in four-part harmony
  • The signinga piece while the register is signed
  • Recessionalas you leave together
  • Receptiondrinks and the wedding breakfast, if you want music to carry on

Pieces couples choose

  • Ave Maria Schubert
  • Ubi Caritas Ola Gjeilo
  • The Lord Bless You and Keep You Rutter
  • Locus Iste Bruckner
  • Panis Angelicus Franck
  • Laudate Dominum Mozart
  • Anima Christi Marco Frisina
  • A Thousand Years arranged for voices

Hymns couples know

Love Divine, All Loves Excelling; Jerusalem; Guide Me, O Thou Great Redeemer; Be Thou My Vision; Lord of All Hopefulness; Dear Lord and Father of Mankind.

At a civil ceremony on approved premises in England and Wales, the registrar keeps religious music out, so hymns and sacred pieces move to the drinks reception. For the ceremony itself we sing secular pieces and songs arranged for voices, such as Make You Feel My Love or Songbird.

Each price covers up to two hours, with a rehearsal before the ceremony, the sheet music and the planning call. Prices are for Greater London; further out, we add travel to your written quote before you book. The quote is the whole cost, and no VAT is added.

Four voices fill most parish churches and ceremony rooms. Six or eight suit a large church or a long guest list.

How to book

  1. Send us your date, your venue and any music you already have in mind.
  2. Within one working day, Luca sends you suggestions, recordings and a written quote.
  3. You sign a booking confirmation and pay in full or in two halves. The first half holds your date; the second is due 48 hours before the wedding.
  4. A week before, you, your venue and your planner receive the running order. On the day, the musicians arrive an hour early.
