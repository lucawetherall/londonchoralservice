The singers arrive in the dress you asked for, sing carols where your guests are standing, and leave you to run the evening. They sing unaccompanied and need only somewhere to stand: a corner of the lobby, the foot of a staircase, or a spot by the bar.

Hear the singers

Four more films from our Christmas sets, sung by the singers you would book.

Ding Dong! Merrily on High — Full Choir

A French dance tune from 1589 with words George Ratcliffe Woodward added in 1924, sung by eight voices. We sing it at markets and in lobbies, where guests can hear the long Gloria runs over the noise.

Once in Royal David’s City — Full Choir

Mrs Alexander’s words of 1848 to Henry Gauntlett’s tune, sung by eight voices. Guests know it from the first verse, and we often open a set at a drinks reception or in a lobby with it.

O Little Town of Bethlehem — Full Choir

Phillips Brooks’s words of 1868 to Forest Green, sung by eight voices. It is slower than most carols, so we use it to bring a party set down before the next lively number.

Winter Wonderland — Full Choir

The 1934 song by Felix Bernard and Richard Smith, sung by eight voices in close harmony. The words are about snow, so we programme it between the carols at receptions.

Frequently asked questions

Can the carol singers move around the room?

Yes. They can roam through the crowd, stopping wherever a group has gathered, or stay in one spot for sets of three or four carols. Roaming suits a drinks reception where guests arrive across an hour; fixed sets suit a seated dinner or a switch-on with a countdown. A standard booking runs up to two hours including breaks, usually three or four sets of about twenty-five minutes.

Can carol singers perform outdoors in the cold?

Yes. Markets, courtyards, and light switch-ons are ordinary December work. We ask for cover from rain and somewhere warm to wait between sets. In a hard frost we sing shorter sets more often.

What do carol singers wear?

Whatever suits your event. Concert dress for a formal evening, all black for a lobby set that should not draw the eye, ordinary clothes for a market or a family party, or Christmas jumpers if you want the singers to look festive. We have Santa hats if you want them.

How many carol singers should we book for our venue?

Size the group to the space and the noise. Four singers suit a lobby, an office reception, or a room of up to 150. Six fill a mid-sized hall or a hotel foyer. Eight hold their own over a busy bar or a crowd talking across them, and twelve are for several hundred guests.

What does the venue need to provide for carol singers?

Somewhere to stand. A high ceiling and a hard floor help, so a staircase, an atrium, or a gallery works well. The singers sing unaccompanied, so there is nothing to plug in. If you want a pianist between the sets, the venue needs a piano; if it has none, we can bring a keyboard and speaker for an extra charge, quoted upfront.

How far ahead should we book carol singers for December?

Most years our December dates start to fill by the middle of September. Late bookings are welcome, and a date booked in December costs the same as one booked in June. Send us the date and the venue, however close, and we will confirm availability within one working day.

Are you available on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day?

Yes, we sing on both. Those two dates carry a premium of around 25%, confirmed in the written quote before you commit. They are the first dates to go each year, so ask early.

How do I hire carol singers?

Send us the date, the venue and roughly how many guests, and we check the date for you. We reply with a written quote within one working day, from £1,150 for four singers for up to two hours. Half the fee as a deposit secures the date, and the rest is due before we sing.

How do we book and pay for carol singers?

Half the fee secures the date and is non-refundable; the rest is due before we sing. You can pay in one go or in two equal instalments, unless the event is ten days or fewer away, when we ask for a single payment. We can invoice a company against its purchase order on 14-day terms, paid before the event.
