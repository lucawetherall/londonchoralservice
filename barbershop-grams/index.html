Birthdays

The surprise birthday gram

You tell us where they will be and when. Four singers arrive, work out which one is the birthday person, and sing Happy Birthday in four-part harmony. Then one song chosen for them from the repertoire, with their name sung into it. The whole visit takes ten minutes.

An open-plan office works. So does a kitchen at half past seven in the morning, a front door on a Saturday, or a restaurant table between the main course and the pudding.

If the birthday falls on a Tuesday and the party is on the Saturday, consider sending us on the Tuesday rather than joining the party.

Fortieths, fiftieths and eightieths get the same four singers. Tell us the age and they will work it into the song, alongside the name.

Love

For someone you love

Valentine’s Day and anniversaries are the obvious ones. An ordinary Thursday makes the better surprise, because nobody is braced for it.

Hello! Ma Baby, Let Me Call You Sweetheart and I Love You Truly are barbershop standards, and all three are in the repertoire. If there is a song that belongs to the two of you and it is not on the list, we arrange it for four voices from £200. Allow a week for the singers to learn it.

Proposals

Proposals

We arrive a few minutes before you ask, sing while you get the ring out of your pocket, and then leave the two of you to it.

Send us the plan a couple of days ahead: where you will be standing, what the signal is, and who else is in on it. The singers wait out of sight until you give the signal.

If it goes well, we also sing weddings.

At work

Leaving dos, promotions and work anniversaries

Four singers across the desk from someone on their last day, in front of the team they are leaving. It takes ten minutes out of a Friday afternoon and the whole floor watches.

A trading floor or an all-hands is a bigger room than a desk. For those we send eight or twelve voices instead of four, quoted for the room.

We invoice the company. Tell us who signs visitors into the building and we will arrange the access with them beforehand.

Logistics

Making sure the surprise lands

We ask you for five things when you book:

  • Someone on the inside who knows: a receptionist, a manager, a flatmate.
  • A ten-minute window when the person will be in one place.
  • Access: building sign-in, a table booking, a gate code.
  • A phone number for the day that is not the recipient’s.
  • Whether you want it filmed, and by whom.

Luca rings whoever is on the inside the day before, so that someone at the other end knows what is coming.

Questions

Questions

How much notice do you need?

48 hours. Valentine's week and December book up well before that, so ask early for those.

Where will you sing?

Anywhere in Greater London we can get four singers into: offices, homes, restaurants, pubs, parks, and care homes. Hospital wards need the ward's permission first, which we will ask for.

What happens if they are not there?

Tell us before the singers set off and we rearrange once at no charge. After they have set off the fee stands, because four singers have given up the slot.

How do you keep it a surprise?

We only ever contact the number you give us. We never ring or email the recipient, and we do not post anything about a booking before it happens.

Can you sing a song that is not on your repertoire list?

Yes. We arrange your song for four voices from £200. Allow a week so the singers can rehearse it properly.

Can we film it?

Yes, and we would like a copy. We ask the person who was surprised for permission before we post anything ourselves.

Is there a cheaper version?

No. A Barbershop Gram is four voices, because four is what makes the harmony. We do hire single singers from £250, but that is a different thing and we do not sell it as a gram.

Who will be singing?

Four singers chosen for your booking by Luca Wetherall, our Artistic Director and Tutor in Music at the University of Oxford, who auditions every musician on the team himself.
