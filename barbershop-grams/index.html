Occasions

What to send a gram for

A Barbershop Gram is a singing telegram with four voices in it rather than one. Ten minutes, wherever the person happens to be. Birthday is the occasion we built it around, and everything else on this list uses the same four singers with a different song at the end.

  • Birthday
  • Valentine’s
  • Proposal
  • Leaving do
  • Anniversary
  • Just because

The singers

Who turns up

Luca Wetherall, our Artistic Director, picks the four singers for every gram himself. He is Tutor in Music at the University of Oxford, and no singer joins the team without auditioning for him first. The four who arrive come out of the same hand-picked group that sings our weddings and our carol services.

Four voices is the standard gram. Where the room is bigger than a floor of desks we send eight or twelve instead, quoted for the occasion.

We will arrange any song at all for four voices from £200, and the singers learn it in 24 hours. That fits inside the usual 48 hours’ notice, so you can book at normal notice and still have your own song arranged. Our repertoire is published in full as well, so you can settle on the song while you are still deciding whether to book.

Ask on WhatsApp and you will usually have an answer, and a price, the same day.

Birthdays

The surprise birthday gram

You tell us where they will be and when. Four singers arrive, find the right person, and sing Happy Birthday in four-part harmony, and then one song chosen for them from the repertoire with their name worked into it. Ten minutes covers all of it, and the singers leave once the song is finished.

An open-plan office works. So does a kitchen at half past seven in the morning, a front door on a Saturday, or a restaurant table between the main course and the pudding. What we need from you is a ten-minute window when the person will be somewhere we can reach them, and someone at that end who knows we are coming.

If the birthday falls on a Tuesday and the party is on the Saturday, consider the Tuesday. At a party we are one act among several and the surprise competes with everything already planned; on the Tuesday there is nothing else booked.

Fortieths, fiftieths and eightieths get the same four singers. Tell us the age and they will work it into the song alongside the name.

Love

For someone you love

Valentine’s Day and anniversaries are the obvious ones, and they are also the days a person half expects something. Send the singers on an ordinary Thursday and nobody is braced for it. Hello! Ma Baby, Let Me Call You Sweetheart and I Love You Truly are barbershop standards and all three are in the repertoire; if there is a song that belongs to the two of you, we will arrange that instead.

It does not have to be romantic. The same four singers and the same ten minutes work for a friend who has had a rough month, or for your parents on a date that is not an anniversary.

Proposals

Asking someone to marry you

We arrive a few minutes before you ask, sing while you get the ring out of your pocket, and then go, so the rest of the evening is yours. Send us the plan a couple of days ahead: where you will be standing, what the signal is, who else is in on it, and whether anybody is filming. The singers wait out of sight until you give the signal.

Restaurants and hotels usually want a word before four strangers start singing in the middle of service. Tell us the venue and we will square it with them ourselves.

At work

Leaving dos, promotions and work anniversaries

Four singers across the desk from someone on their last day, in front of the team they are leaving. We sing where they sit, so nobody has to be manoeuvred into a meeting room on a pretext, and the whole thing takes ten minutes out of a Friday afternoon. Tell us who signs visitors into the building and we will arrange the access with them beforehand.

A trading floor, or an all-hands with two hundred people in it, needs more than four voices, and for those we send eight or twelve, quoted for the occasion. We invoice the company rather than an individual.

Logistics

Making sure the surprise lands

Luca rings whoever is on the inside the day before and walks them through it: where the singers will wait, when they will move, and what to do if the person has gone out for a coffee. The four arrive early and stay out of sight until the moment. Every call and message about the booking goes to you, and we post nothing anywhere until it has happened.

From your end:

  • Someone on the inside who knows: a receptionist, a manager, a flatmate.
  • A ten-minute window when the person will be in one place.
  • Access: building sign-in, a table booking, a gate code.
  • A phone number for the day that is not the recipient’s.
  • Whether you want it filmed, and by whom.

Questions

Before you book

How much notice do you need?

48 hours. For Valentine’s week, ask us further ahead than that. For December, ask earlier again: most years our dates have gone by the middle of September.

Where will you sing?

Anywhere in Greater London we can get four singers into: offices, homes, restaurants, pubs, parks, and care homes. Hospital wards need the ward’s permission first, which we will ask for. We sing outside Greater London too, with the travel costed in the written quote before you commit.

What happens if they are not there?

Tell us before the singers set off and we rearrange once at no charge. After they have set off the fee stands, because four singers have given up the slot.

How do you keep it a surprise?

We only ever contact the number you give us. We never ring or email the recipient, and we do not post anything about a booking before it happens.

Can you sing a song that is not on your repertoire list?

Yes. We arrange your song for four voices from £200. The singers learn it in 24 hours, inside the usual 48 hours’ notice.

Can we film it?

Yes, and we would like a copy. We ask the person who was surprised for permission before we post anything ourselves.

Is there a cheaper version?

No. A Barbershop Gram is four voices, because four is what makes the harmony. We do hire single singers from £250, but that is a different thing and we do not sell it as a gram.

Who will be singing?

Four singers chosen for your booking by Luca Wetherall, our Artistic Director and Tutor in Music at the University of Oxford, who auditions every musician on the team himself.
