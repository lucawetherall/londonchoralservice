What we provide

Luca Wetherall, our Artistic Director, plans the music with you from your first call. Some couples arrive with a full shortlist — Zadok for the processional, Ave Maria for the register, the Widor Toccata on the way out. Others have no idea where to start. Both are fine. Luca sends you recordings of the pieces on the table and you narrow it down from there.

Our repertoire covers classical, sacred, and contemporary music. If you want a Bach chorale, we play it. If you want A Thousand Years sung by four voices, we arrange it. Bespoke arrangements are included at no extra charge.

Our singers and instrumentalists cover the processional, hymns, register signing, and recessional. We can carry on through the drinks reception and the wedding breakfast if you want music running across the whole day.

You receive a confirmed running order a week before the wedding. On the day, musicians arrive an hour before the ceremony to set up and coordinate with your planner or venue manager. Book early to secure your date, though we do take late bookings when we can.
