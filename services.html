Music for funerals, weddings, and ceremonies

From the most solemn farewells to the most joyful celebrations, we provide live music that rises to the occasion.

Funerals

The right music brings comfort that words alone cannot. Our singers lead congregational hymns with warmth and authority, giving everyone present the confidence to join in. Between hymns, solo pieces and choral works create moments of stillness and reflection.

What we provide

Every funeral booking includes a personal consultation with Luca Wetherall, our Artistic Director. He will talk through your music choices, suggest repertoire that suits the tone you want, and handle coordination with your funeral director and officiant. You receive a confirmed running order at least 48 hours before the service.

On the day, our singers arrive 45 minutes before the service begins. They rehearse in the venue, check acoustics, and confirm positions with the officiant. During the service, they lead up to three congregational hymns and perform up to three choral or solo pieces. After the service, they can provide reflective music as mourners leave.

From first contact to the day

You get in touch by phone, email, or through our contact form. Luca calls you back within a few hours to discuss the occasion, the venue, and your music preferences. He then sends a written proposal with repertoire suggestions and a clear quote. Once you confirm, we handle everything else. Most families complete the whole process in two or three short conversations.

We perform in churches, crematoriums, chapels, synagogues, and outdoor spaces across the UK. Read our guide to music at crematoriums if your service is at a crematorium. For help choosing hymns and songs, see our funeral music guide, our list of popular funeral hymns, or our guide to non-religious funeral music.

Weddings

Live music turns a ceremony into something people remember for years. Our singers and instrumentalists perform during the processional, hymns, signing of the register, and recessional. We can continue through drinks and the wedding breakfast if you want a single musical thread running through the whole day.

What we provide

Every wedding booking begins with a conversation about your vision for the day. Luca Wetherall, our Artistic Director, helps you choose repertoire that fits the tone you want. You might know you want "Zadok the Priest" for the processional, or you might have no idea where to start. Either way, Luca walks you through the options and sends you recordings of suggested pieces.

Our repertoire spans classical, sacred, and contemporary music. If there is a piece you have always imagined hearing on your wedding day, whether it is a Bach chorale or a pop song, we can arrange it for your chosen ensemble. We prepare custom arrangements at no extra charge.

From first contact to the day

You get in touch and tell us your wedding date, venue, and any initial music ideas. Luca sends a proposal with ensemble options, repertoire suggestions, and a clear quote. After you confirm, he finalises the programme with you over email or a short call. Our musicians receive the confirmed running order a week before the wedding. On the day, they arrive an hour before the ceremony to set up and coordinate with your wedding planner or venue manager.

Memorials & celebrations of life

A memorial or celebration of life should reflect the person being honoured. Some families want reflective, sacred music. Others want something uplifting and personal. Many want both. We shape the music around the story you want to tell.

What we provide

Luca Wetherall talks with you about the person being remembered, the setting, and the atmosphere you want to create. He suggests repertoire that fits. If the person loved jazz, we can arrange a jazz standard for vocal ensemble. If they sang in a church choir, we can perform the pieces they loved. You receive a full programme in advance with every piece, every cue point, and every transition mapped out.

We perform in churches, community halls, hotels, private homes, gardens, marquees, and outdoor spaces. We bring portable amplification when the venue needs it. If you can hold a gathering there, we can perform there.

From first contact to the day

You tell us about the person and the occasion. Luca sends repertoire suggestions within 24 hours and a written quote. Once confirmed, he coordinates timing with your celebrant or officiant. Our musicians arrive 45 minutes early to rehearse in the space, check sight lines, and confirm positions. After the event, several families have told us they received comments about the music for weeks afterwards.

Corporate events & ceremonies

Award ceremonies, gala dinners, commemorative services, product launches, and carol concerts all gain weight and atmosphere from live music. We perform at corporate events for law firms, banks, charities, schools, and City livery companies across London and the UK.

What we provide

We tailor the ensemble and repertoire to your event. A carol concert might call for a full choir of eight singers with an organist. A drinks reception might suit a vocal quartet performing a mix of classical and popular music. A formal dinner might need a soloist for one set piece. Luca Wetherall discusses your event with you or your event coordinator, then sends a proposal with ensemble options and a programme outline.

From first contact to the day

You share the event details: date, venue, format, and any music preferences. We send a proposal and quote within 48 hours. Once confirmed, Luca finalises the programme and coordinates arrival times, positioning, and technical requirements with your venue or event manager. Our musicians arrive in concert dress, set up without fuss, and perform to the brief.

Our ensembles

Soloist
A single, exceptional voice for intimate settings and moments that call for simplicity and purity. One to three performance pieces, plus leading congregation through up to three hymns.
Small Choir (4 singers)
Four-part harmony that fills a room with warmth and depth without overwhelming the space. Ideal for mid-sized venues and ceremonies of 50-200 people. One to three performance pieces, plus leading congregation through up to three hymns.
Quintet (5 singers)
Adding a second soprano to the choral quartet makes a huge difference to the sound in your venue and can really help lead the congregation in their singing. It also allows for much more freedom of repertoire as many pieces are written with two soprano parts. One to three performance pieces, plus leading congregation through up to three hymns.
Sextet (6 singers)
One of our most cost effective options if you want the sound of a big choir for less. A far bigger sound than a quartet, providing a musical boost to any service. One to three performance pieces, plus leading congregation through up to three hymns.
Full Choir (8 singers)
A richer, more resonant sound for larger venues and occasions that need greater presence. Powerful without dominating the space. One to three performance pieces, plus leading congregation through up to three hymns.
Chorus (12 singers)
The full choral experience: a powerful, moving ensemble that can fill a cathedral and create moments people never forget. One to four performance pieces, plus leading congregation through up to four hymns.

Frequently asked questions

What types of occasions do you provide music for?

We provide live music for funerals, weddings, memorials, celebrations of life, corporate events, and ceremonies of all kinds across the United Kingdom. That includes religious services, civil ceremonies, humanist celebrations, and private gatherings. If your occasion does not fit a standard category, get in touch. We have performed at naming ceremonies, anniversary services, charity galas, and school events.

What ensemble sizes are available?

We offer a soloist (single voice), small choir (4 singers), quintet (5 singers), sextet (6 singers), full choir (8 singers), and chorus (12 singers). We also provide organists, pianists, harpists, and string quartets. The right ensemble depends on your venue size, your budget, and the sound you want. A soloist suits an intimate crematorium chapel. A full choir fills a large parish church. Luca will recommend the best option when you get in touch.

Do you perform at crematoriums?

Yes. Our musicians perform at crematoriums across the UK. Crematorium services are shorter than church services, so we adapt our programme to fit the time available. We typically perform one or two pieces and lead one or two hymns. Our singers project well in smaller spaces without overwhelming the room. Read our guide to crematorium music for more detail on what works well in these settings.

Can you arrange any piece of music?

Our repertoire spans classical, sacred, and contemporary music. If there is a piece you want to hear, whether it is a Bach chorale, a folk song, or a pop ballad, we can arrange it for your chosen ensemble. Luca prepares custom arrangements at no extra charge. He will send you a recording of the arrangement before the day so you can hear how it sounds. The only limitation is copyright: some publishers restrict live performance of certain works, but this affects very few pieces.
