Choirs for Harrow-on-the-Hill, Pinner and Stanmore

Musicians selected by Luca Wetherall, Artistic Director & Tutor in Music, University of Oxford — read more about our team.

Harrow sits on the north-western edge of the capital, under the spire of St Mary’s at Harrow-on-the-Hill. Our conservatoire-trained singers and instrumentalists work at funerals, weddings, memorials and ceremonies on the Hill, at Breakspear Crematorium, and in the parish churches of Pinner and Stanmore.

Weddings at St Mary’s Harrow-on-the-Hill

St Mary’s Harrow-on-the-Hill, perched above the surrounding streets, is one of north-west London’s most atmospheric wedding venues. Its fine acoustic rewards choral singing, and our ensembles know the building. We also sing weddings at churches in Pinner and Stanmore, and at licensed venues for civil ceremonies. For help choosing hymns and anthems that reflect your taste, take a look at our guide to choosing wedding hymns.

Choir sizes from the Hill to Breakspear Crematorium

St Mary’s Harrow-on-the-Hill has a fine acoustic that rewards more voices. For a wedding there a sextet usually feels right, with a full eight-voice choir a strong choice when the congregation is large. Breakspear Crematorium runs to a fixed service length, so a quartet is generally the right call: large enough to lead a hymn, compact enough to fit the chapel without crowding the family. The parish churches of Pinner, Stanmore, Kenton, and Wealdstone vary in size; most sit comfortably with a quartet, while a sextet adds weight to processional hymns at the larger ones. For a graveside committal, a soloist tends to suit the occasion better than a larger group. Our pricing page lays out costs.

Funerals in Harrow, Pinner and Stanmore

We sing at venues throughout the borough, from St Mary’s Harrow-on-the-Hill, a church with roots in the eleventh century, to Breakspear Crematorium, which serves families from across north-west London. Our singers also perform at parish churches in Pinner, Stanmore, Kenton, and Wealdstone, adapting their repertoire to reflect the wishes of each family. Our funeral music guide covers the options, from traditional hymns and a choral psalm to a contemporary song arranged for voices. Please get in touch whenever you are ready to discuss your plans.

Harrow’s diverse community means that our singers are experienced in serving families of many faiths and traditions. We sing at Church of England funerals, secular celebrations of life and memorial ceremonies. We agree timings with funeral directors and clergy, and arrive with time to prepare. For more detail on how we work, visit our services page.

Carol evenings near Harrow-on-the-Hill

Harrow’s offices tend to want a full carol programme, residents near St Mary’s Harrow-on-the-Hill often prefer singers who move through an evening, and venues across Pinner and Stanmore ask for either. Our professional carol singers perform both formats, or you can hire carol singers for the roaming version.

Questions about Harrow-on-the-Hill and Breakspear

How many singers suit a wedding at St Mary’s Harrow-on-the-Hill?

A sextet (from £1,600) usually feels right, because the church’s fine acoustic rewards more voices. When the congregation is large, a full choir of eight (from £2,000) is a strong choice.

Can we have live singing at Breakspear Crematorium?

Yes. Breakspear Crematorium is the principal crematorium serving Harrow and north-west London, and our singers know its chapel well. We liaise with crematorium staff and your funeral director so the music fits the service timings.

What would you sing at St Mary’s Harrow-on-the-Hill, and what at Breakspear?

At St Mary’s Harrow-on-the-Hill, the ancient setting lends itself to traditional hymns, psalms, and choral anthems. At Breakspear Crematorium or the borough’s smaller churches in Kenton and Wealdstone, we often perform contemporary songs and reflective pieces. We can prepare bespoke arrangements of a particular song, which may carry an extra charge.
