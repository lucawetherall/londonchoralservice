Funeral and wedding choirs in Havering

Musicians selected by Luca Wetherall, Artistic Director & Tutor in Music, University of Oxford — read more about our team.

Havering sits on London’s eastern edge, where the city gives way to the green belt countryside of Essex. From the market town character of Romford and the village charm of Upminster to the quieter streets of Hornchurch and Corbets Tey, we provide professional singers and choirs for funerals, weddings, memorials, and ceremonies across the borough.

Funerals at South Essex Crematorium and St Andrew’s Hornchurch

South Essex Crematorium in Corbets Tey is the principal crematorium serving families in Havering and the surrounding area. Its chapels are well suited to live music, and our singers have performed there on many occasions, adapting their repertoire to reflect the full range of musical tastes — from traditional hymns and psalm settings to popular songs and modern classical works. St Andrew’s Church in Hornchurch, a handsome medieval parish church with a fine acoustic, is another venue we know well. Its spacious nave allows choral sound to develop naturally, creating a moving atmosphere for funeral and memorial services. For help with choosing the right music, please see our funeral music guide or get in touch directly.

If the service is at South Essex Crematorium in Corbets Tey, read our guide to crematorium music on timing and acoustics in a shorter chapel service.

We also regularly provide funeral singers for services in Romford, Upminster, and the smaller churches and chapels that serve Havering’s communities. In a large parish church with a pipe organ or a modern crematorium chapel, we work closely with funeral directors, clergy, and families so the music is handled with care. Our singers are comfortable in services of all faiths and denominations, and we are happy to prepare unfamiliar repertoire when requested. Our services page describes the full range, from a graveside soloist in Upminster to a choir in a Romford church.

Choir sizes for Hornchurch, Upminster and Romford

St Andrew’s Hornchurch is a sizeable parish church with a generous nave, and a sextet usually carries congregational hymns well there for a wedding — with a full eight-voice choir worth considering for ceremonies where the congregation is large. The parish churches of Upminster and Romford handle a quartet or sextet comfortably depending on the scale of the day. For a smaller funeral with family-only present, a quartet generally suits better than a larger ensemble. If the service is taking place in a modern crematorium chapel, a quartet is usually the right call: enough to lead a hymn, compact enough to fit the room. For a graveside or short committal, a soloist is usually plenty. Our pricing page sets out costs by ensemble size.

Havering funeral questions

Do you cover crematorium services in Havering?

Yes. South Essex Crematorium in Corbets Tey is the main crematorium serving Havering, and our singers have performed in its chapels on many occasions. We coordinate with crematorium staff and your funeral director so the music fits naturally within the service timings.

How much is a funeral choir at South Essex Crematorium or St Andrew’s Hornchurch?

For services at South Essex Crematorium in Corbets Tey, St Andrew’s Hornchurch, or churches in Romford and Upminster, a soloist starts from £250. A small choir of four is £1,150, a quintet £1,400, a sextet £1,600, and a full choir of eight £2,000. Havering is a Greater London borough, so the fee includes getting our singers to Romford, Hornchurch or Upminster.

What size of choir suits a family-only funeral in Havering?

For a smaller funeral with only family present, a quartet (from £1,150) usually suits better than a larger ensemble. At a graveside or short committal, a soloist (from £250) is usually plenty.

Can you sing popular songs at South Essex Crematorium as well as hymns at St Andrew’s Hornchurch?

At St Andrew’s Hornchurch, the spacious nave brings out the best in traditional hymns, psalms, and choral anthems. At South Essex Crematorium, we frequently perform popular songs, contemporary pieces, and modern classical works. We can prepare bespoke arrangements of any song — if it mattered to your loved one, we will make it work for voices.

Four of our guides help with planning in Havering: the funeral music guide and popular funeral hymns for a service at South Essex Crematorium, the funeral music costs guide for fees, and the wedding hymns guide for a wedding at St Andrew’s Hornchurch.

Weddings at St Andrew’s Hornchurch

Havering’s churches offer some appealing wedding settings. St Andrew’s Hornchurch, with its ancient tower and churchyard, is a popular choice, while the churches of Upminster and Romford each have their own character. A live choir does what a recording cannot: hold the hymns together for a congregation that is unsure of the tune, and follow them with a piece during the signing of the register that guests will go on talking about over dinner. Our singers are experienced at leading congregational hymns and equally at home performing more intricate choral works. Our guide to choosing wedding hymns can help you pick hymns a St Andrew’s congregation can follow.

Carols in Romford and Upminster

Offices in Havering usually want a seated carol evening, residents near St Andrew’s Hornchurch often prefer singers who circulate through a party, and venues in Romford and Upminster ask for both. We provide singers for a December evening built around a running order, or you can hire carol singers to roam a reception instead.
