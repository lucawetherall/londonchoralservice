Funeral and wedding choirs in Westminster

Musicians selected by Luca Wetherall, Artistic Director & Tutor in Music, University of Oxford — read more about our team.

The City of Westminster is home to some of Britain’s most celebrated churches and ceremonial spaces. We provide professional singers and choirs for funerals, weddings, and memorial services at venues throughout the borough.

Funeral music in Westminster

Westminster Abbey hosts state funerals and large-scale memorial services, and several of our singers hold positions in its choral foundation. We understand the Abbey’s protocols and can provide additional voices to supplement its own choir when families require a larger ensemble. St Margaret’s Westminster, the parish church of the House of Commons next door, seats around 500 and suits both grand tributes and more personal farewells.

The Guards’ Chapel at Wellington Barracks on Birdcage Walk is the principal place of worship for the Household Division, and we regularly sing at military funerals and memorial services there. Its compact, modern interior (rebuilt after a wartime bombing) produces a clear, focused sound well suited to a quartet or sextet. St James’s Piccadilly, Christopher Wren’s only West End church, has a bright acoustic and a fine organ. We also sing at St Clement Danes on the Strand, the central church of the Royal Air Force, and at the Savoy Chapel on Savoy Hill, a small Chapel Royal often chosen for private family services.

For Catholic funerals, Westminster Cathedral on Victoria Street is the mother church of the Catholic faith in England and Wales. Its Byzantine-inspired interior and professional choir set a high standard, and our singers are accustomed to working within its liturgical framework for requiem masses. Families who want help selecting music for any of these venues can consult our funeral music guide or browse our list of popular funeral hymns.

Logistics in central Westminster

Parking near Westminster’s churches is limited and expensive. We advise funeral directors to arrange drop-off points for musicians rather than relying on on-street parking. Our singers travel by public transport whenever possible. Westminster Abbey, St Margaret’s, and the Guards’ Chapel all sit within walking distance of St James’s Park station. The Savoy Chapel is a short walk from Temple or Embankment. We arrive at least 45 minutes before every service to warm up and coordinate with clergy and venue staff.

Westminster’s venues often have specific requirements around security, access times, and rehearsal space. The Abbey and Cathedral both run on tight schedules, and we know how to work within them. The Guards’ Chapel requires advance clearance through the Household Division. We handle these logistics directly so families and funeral directors do not have to.

Families arranging services in neighbouring Kensington & Chelsea or across the river in Lambeth may also find our singers a good fit. We cover both boroughs and can advise on venue-specific considerations. For services in the City of London, just east of Westminster, we are equally available.

Memorial services and celebrations of life

Westminster is a common choice for large memorial services held weeks or months after a funeral. These events often take place at Westminster Abbey, St Martin-in-the-Fields, or St James’s Piccadilly and can involve more ambitious musical programmes than a funeral allows. We provide choirs of eight to twelve voices for memorial services and can include orchestral players if the family wants a full performance of a Fauré Requiem or other large-scale work. Our memorial service planning guide covers the practical steps involved.

Wedding choirs in Westminster

St Margaret’s Westminster has hosted weddings for centuries, including those of Samuel Pepys, Winston Churchill, and many members of Parliament. Couples who marry there can draw on the church’s strong musical tradition, and we provide choirs of four to twelve voices to match the scale of the occasion. St James’s Piccadilly is another popular choice, with its generous nave and welcoming atmosphere.

We work with each couple to plan the music from first enquiry through to the day itself. That includes hymns for the congregation, an anthem or motet during the signing of the register, and processional and recessional music. Our guide to choosing wedding hymns covers the most requested options. We also provide singers for civil ceremonies at Westminster venues licensed for marriage, including hotel function rooms and private members’ clubs.

Ensemble sizes and pricing

Westminster Abbey and Westminster Cathedral call for larger ensembles of six to twelve voices. Smaller churches like the Savoy Chapel or the Guards’ Chapel work well with a quartet. A solo singer suits an intimate memorial or a registry office ceremony. We help you match the ensemble to the space and the budget. Full details are on our pricing page.

Christmas carol singers in Westminster

Each December we provide carol services and roaming carol singers for offices, hotels, and residents’ events across Westminster, from the West End church of St James’s Piccadilly to the hotels and function rooms around Mayfair and Park Lane. A choir can lead a full carol service at your workplace or a church you have hired, or you can book roaming carol singers for a residents’ or corporate gathering. For hotels arranging a festive reception, our page on carol singers for hotels covers formats and timings.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a funeral singer cost in Westminster?

A solo funeral singer in Westminster starts from £250. A small choir of four comes to £1,150, a quintet £1,400, a sextet £1,600, and a full choir of eight £2,000. Prices include travel within Greater London and liaison with your funeral director. For services at Westminster Abbey or Westminster Cathedral, we may recommend a larger ensemble to suit the acoustic.

Can you provide a choir for a wedding at St Margaret’s Westminster?

Yes. We provide wedding choirs of four to twelve voices at St Margaret’s and other Westminster churches. We coordinate with the church’s own organist and verger, handle all rehearsal scheduling, and supply printed orders of service with hymn texts if needed.

What types of music can you perform at a funeral in Westminster?

Traditional hymns, sacred choral works, contemporary songs, and popular favourites. We also arrange bespoke versions of any piece a family requests. Our funeral music guide lists the pieces we perform most often, and our crematorium music guide covers shorter services with fixed time slots.

Do you sing at crematoriums near Westminster?

Westminster itself has no crematorium, but we regularly sing at Mortlake Crematorium in Richmond and at Putney Vale. Both are accessible from central London. Our singers adapt to the tighter timings of crematorium services. For guidance on music choices for these venues, see our crematorium music guide.

Do you provide carol singers for Christmas events in Westminster?

Yes. We provide carol services and roaming carol singers for offices, hotels, and residents’ events across Westminster throughout December. The choir performs entirely a cappella, singing a set programme or circulating among guests as the occasion requires. December dates are in especially high demand and usually booked out by mid-September, so early enquiries are essential. See our Christmas page.
