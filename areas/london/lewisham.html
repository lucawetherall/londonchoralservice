Funeral and wedding choirs in Lewisham

The London Borough of Lewisham encompasses the diverse communities of Lewisham, Catford, Blackheath, and Grove Park. We provide professional singers and choirs for funerals, weddings, memorials, and ceremonies at churches, crematoria, and cemeteries throughout the borough.

Funeral music in Lewisham

Lewisham’s crematoria and churches are places where families gather to say goodbye, and our role is to ensure the music honours the occasion with dignity and beauty. Hither Green Crematorium is one of South London’s busiest and most well-known crematoria, and we have provided singers there on many occasions, understanding the precise timings and gentle approach that crematorium services require. Lewisham Crematorium, located in the south of the borough, offers a similarly peaceful setting for farewell services. At both venues, even a single trained voice can bring a remarkable depth of feeling to a short ceremony. For advice on choosing suitable music, please see our funeral music guide or get in touch to talk through your wishes.

For practical guidance on music at crematorium services — timing, acoustics, and making a shorter service meaningful — see our guide to crematorium music.

For church funerals, St Mary’s Lewisham provides a handsome and welcoming space with a long history of serving the local community. Grove Park Cemetery is another venue where families often request live music for graveside services or committal rites, and our singers are experienced in performing outdoors with poise and sensitivity. We liaise directly with funeral directors and clergy to ensure that every practical detail is taken care of, leaving you free to focus on what matters most. You can read more about our approach on our services page.

Wedding choirs in Lewisham

A grand celebration at one of Lewisham’s larger churches, or an intimate ceremony in a smaller parish setting — a live choir does what a recording cannot: lead a congregation through a hymn most of them only half-remember, and follow it with a piece during the signing of the register guests will go on talking about over dinner. The churches of Blackheath and Lee offer particularly attractive venues, with fine acoustics and beautiful interiors. Our singers will work with you to create a programme of hymns, anthems, and choral pieces that reflects your personality and the spirit of the day. For ideas on building the perfect musical programme, explore our guide to choosing wedding hymns.

Ensembles for weddings, funerals, and memorials

St Mary’s Lewisham is a substantial parish church, and a sextet usually carries a wedding there confidently — with a full eight-voice choir worth considering when the congregation is large and the music programme leans on processional hymns. The churches around Blackheath and Lee tend to have fine acoustics that reward a sextet for weddings, while a quartet works well for smaller services. For a graveside service at Grove Park Cemetery, a soloist or duo is often more fitting than a larger ensemble: a clear unaccompanied voice carries outdoors well and suits the simplicity of the moment. For chapel committals, a quartet keeps the music compact within the timing. We’re happy to talk it through once we know the venue. Our pricing page sets out costs.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a funeral singer cost in Lewisham?

For services at Hither Green Crematorium, Lewisham Crematorium, or St Mary’s Lewisham, a soloist starts from £215. A small choir of four starts from £1,150, a quintet from £1,400, a sextet from £1,600, and a full choir of eight from £2,000. All prices include travel within Greater London.

Can you provide a choir for a wedding in Lewisham?

Yes. The churches of Blackheath and Lee have particularly fine acoustics for choral singing, and St Mary’s Lewisham provides a handsome traditional setting. We offer ensembles from a solo vocalist to a twelve-voice choir and handle all the musical planning with the church and your wedding coordinator.

What types of music can you perform at a funeral in Lewisham?

Traditional hymns, classical sacred music, contemporary songs, and popular favourites. At Hither Green and Lewisham crematoria we tailor the programme to fit the allotted service time, and at St Mary’s Lewisham we can draw on a wider choral repertoire. Bespoke arrangements are available on request.

Do you cover crematorium services in Lewisham?

Yes. We sing regularly at both Hither Green Crematorium and Lewisham Crematorium. Our singers understand the fixed timings these chapels require and coordinate directly with staff to ensure the music runs seamlessly. We also provide singers for graveside services at Grove Park Cemetery.
