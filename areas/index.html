Areas we serve across the United Kingdom

Based in London, available nationwide. Our conservatoire-trained singers, choirs, and instrumentalists perform at funerals, weddings, memorials, and ceremonies in every corner of England — from cathedral cities to village churches, crematoriums to private estates.

How our coverage works

The London boroughs

All 32 London boroughs, plus the City of London, have their own page, linked from the main London entry below. Each borough page names the specific venues we sing in — parish churches, crematoriums, registry offices, hotels, livery halls, college chapels. So if you’re booking in Westminster, you’ll see Westminster Abbey, St Margaret’s, the Grosvenor Chapel, and the registry offices we work in regularly. Southwark, Camden, Kensington & Chelsea, and Wandsworth work the same way. The venues named are buildings we have sung in or know, not generic place-holders. If you have a specific church or crematorium in mind, the borough page is the fastest way to confirm we know it.

Cities outside London

For Manchester, Liverpool, Cambridge, Oxford, Birmingham, Bath, and the rest, the same musicians travel from London. For closer cities — Cambridge, Oxford, Reading, Brighton, Canterbury — a 90-minute door-to-door is routine and we can usually take a booking on the same lead time as a London one. For further cities like Manchester, Liverpool, and Chester, plan for two to three weeks’ notice so we can co-ordinate travel and overnight stays where needed.

Travel costs

Inside Greater London there is no travel charge. Outside London the travel fee is itemised on the quote: it reflects mileage and time per musician, itemised in your written quote so you can see exactly what you’re paying for. The calculation is the same for a soloist or a full eight-voice choir; what changes is the number of people travelling. Headline prices sit on our pricing page.

Borough page or city page — which to use

If your event is in London, start with the borough page: it tells you which venues we sing in regularly and gives you a sense of the local pattern. If you’re booking outside London, start with the city page — Manchester, Oxford, Cambridge, and so on. The city pages cover lead times, the venues we work in across that region, and what the booking looks like end-to-end. The borough pages sit underneath the London entry; they’re not separate from it.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you arrange singers in cities outside London?

Two to three weeks is comfortable for Manchester, Liverpool, and Birmingham. For closer cities — Cambridge, Oxford, Reading, Brighton — one week is usually enough. We’ve done short-notice bookings in further cities under exceptional circumstances; ask.

Do you charge travel for outer cities?

No travel cost within Greater London. For cities outside London, travel is itemised on the quote and reflects mileage and time per musician. The rate is the same whether the booking is a soloist or a full choir; the variable is the number of musicians travelling.

Can you cover venues outside the listed cities?

Yes. The pages we list are the cities we’re most regularly booked in. We sing UK-wide; if your venue isn’t on the list, get in touch and we’ll work out the travel logistics.

How do you choose musicians for each location?

London is our base, though several of our singers live elsewhere in the country. We send the singers best suited to your music and venue rather than whoever is nearest. The travel decision is ours, and the cost is set out in your written quote.

Don’t see your area listed?

We perform across the whole of the United Kingdom. The cities above are where we receive the most enquiries, but our musicians are available for any venue, anywhere. If your occasion is elsewhere, get in touch and we’ll confirm availability and provide a quote.
