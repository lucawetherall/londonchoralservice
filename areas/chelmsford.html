Chelmsford choirs for the cathedral, crematoriums and Essex villages

Musicians selected by Luca Wetherall, Artistic Director & Tutor in Music, University of Oxford — read more about our team.

Chelmsford is the county town of Essex, about half an hour from London by train, and families and friends can reach it from across the south-east. We send professional singers, choirs, and instrumentalists to the two crematoriums and the parish churches of Chelmsford and the wider county, and, with the cathedral’s permission, to Chelmsford Cathedral.

Funeral music in Chelmsford

Arranging a funeral in the early days of grief is a hard ask, and we can take the music off your hands. Our singers perform at funerals and memorial services throughout Chelmsford and Essex: at Chelmsford Cathedral, the seat of the Bishop of Chelmsford, whose origins reach back to the thirteenth century and where we sing with the cathedral’s permission; at Chelmsford Crematorium; at South Essex Crematorium; and at parish churches across the county. The music might be a single voice singing The Lord Is My Shepherd in a small chapel of rest, or a full choir leading a congregation in a well-known hymn.

Our first conversation with you is about the music that fits the person being remembered; it does not need to be grand. All our musicians are conservatoire-trained, and many have sung in cathedral and collegiate choirs. If you are not sure what to choose, our funeral music guide sets out the practical choices alongside suggestions for hymns, solos, and choral works. You are welcome to message us on WhatsApp or by email and talk things through at your own pace, with no obligation.

Funeral and wedding choir prices in Chelmsford

A choir of four, at £1,150, can lead the hymns in an Essex village church. A quintet costs £1,400, a sextet £1,600, and a choir of eight £2,000, and a chorus of twelve, at £3,000, suits a large service at Chelmsford Cathedral, with the cathedral’s permission. Soloists, organists, harpists, and string quartets are available too. We pick each singer and instrumentalist from a small professional team. Tell us the church, from the cathedral to a parish in the Essex villages, and how the service will run, and we will recommend a group and send a written quote. Each standard package is set out on our pricing page.

Chelmsford Cathedral, the crematoriums, and Hylands

Chelmsford Cathedral was the town’s parish church until the Diocese of Chelmsford was created in 1914. It has two organs by Mander: a four-manual nave organ of forty stops, built in 1994 under the tower, and a two-manual chancel organ of 1995 that keeps pipework from an 1844 instrument. Both can be played from one console. The cathedral launched its own choral foundation in 1995. With the cathedral’s permission our singers can join its choir or sing as a separate ensemble, and a choir of six suits most funerals there.

Chelmsford Crematorium, on Writtle Road, has two chapels. The North Chapel seats 30, with standing room for 8 in the foyer; the South Chapel seats 100, with room for 25 more standing. Both have an electric organ, and the funeral director arranges the organist, who can be ours at £250 alongside a choir. A soloist or four voices suits the North Chapel, and the South Chapel takes a choir of four to six.

South Essex Crematorium is at Corbets Tey, near Upminster, and Havering Council runs it. Its South Chapel seats 90 downstairs and 120 with the glass balcony, and the East Chapel seats 40. Services there are booked as 40 or 60 minutes. Our crematorium music guide explains how the music fits a timed service, and our funeral choir hire page covers how we work with funeral directors.

Wedding choirs and singers

Couples in Chelmsford choose between the cathedral, with its stained glass (where we sing with the cathedral’s permission), and the medieval parish churches in the villages and market towns around the city. Our singers lead the hymns so the congregation can join in with confidence. Signing the register takes around five minutes, which leaves room for one piece: a soprano in a village church, or the full choir beneath the cathedral’s stained glass. We sing at church and civil weddings across Chelmsford and Essex, and we will learn new repertoire if a piece holds particular meaning for you. Our guide to choosing wedding hymns is a good place to start.

For a civil ceremony, Hylands House, on the council-run Hylands Estate, holds ceremonies in its Drawing Room for up to 80 guests. Music at a civil ceremony must have no religious content, so there we sing secular part-songs and arrangements of love songs, and our guide to choral music for a humanist wedding covers the secular repertoire.

Travel and timings for Chelmsford

Greater Anglia trains from London Liverpool Street reach Chelmsford in about half an hour, so our singers travel out on the day of the service. Our prices include travel within Greater London. Chelmsford is outside it, so we add travel to the written quote, and you see the figure before you book. South Essex Crematorium is the exception: Corbets Tey lies in the London Borough of Havering, inside Greater London, so travel there is in the fee.

The singers arrive an hour before the service. In the cathedral or a parish church they rehearse in the building and agree the order of service with the verger or funeral director. At the crematoriums, where one service follows another, they warm up beforehand and confirm the start time with the staff.

Christmas carol singers in Chelmsford

In December we sing for offices and hotels in Chelmsford and for residents’ associations across Essex. We provide singers for your December event, as a roaming set through a building or as carol singers for hire in a fixed group, and we sing a cappella.

Frequently asked questions

What do you charge for a funeral choir in Chelmsford?

We charge £1,150 for four voices at a funeral in Chelmsford, whether at Chelmsford Crematorium, South Essex Crematorium, or a parish church. Five voices are £1,400, six £1,600, and eight £2,000; our organist, added to the choir, is £250. Up to two hours is included, and so is a rehearsal before the service. Most of Essex is beyond Greater London, so travel is quoted on its own and confirmed before you book. Our funeral choir page has more detail.

Would a wedding choir suit Chelmsford Cathedral?

Yes. With the cathedral’s permission, we sing at Chelmsford Cathedral, whose stained glass and warm acoustic suit a wedding choir, and we also sing in the medieval parish churches of the Essex countryside. We provide ensembles from a single vocalist to a chorus of twelve and will learn a piece that holds particular meaning for you.

Can a choir sing at a civil wedding at Hylands House?

Yes. Hylands House holds ceremonies in its Drawing Room for up to 80 guests. Music at a civil ceremony must be secular, so we sing love songs and part-songs in place of hymns, and you send the music list to the registrar beforehand.

Can you sing Pie Jesu or a favourite song at a Chelmsford funeral?

Traditional hymns such as The Lord Is My Shepherd sit alongside classical sacred solos such as Pie Jesu and Ave Maria in our regular repertoire, and we sing contemporary songs and personal favourites too. We will talk with you about music that fits the person being remembered, and we can write an arrangement, which may carry an extra charge.

Do you sing at Chelmsford Crematorium and South Essex Crematorium?

Yes. Chelmsford Crematorium and South Essex Crematorium are the two we visit most often, though we cover crematoriums across the county. Chelmsford has a North Chapel for 30 and a South Chapel for 100; South Essex has a South Chapel for up to 120, balcony included, and an East Chapel for 40. Our singers coordinate with the staff and your funeral director so the music fits the order of service.

Is travel included for a funeral at South Essex Crematorium?

Yes. South Essex Crematorium at Corbets Tey is in the London Borough of Havering, inside Greater London, where our prices include travel. For Chelmsford Crematorium, the cathedral, and other venues in Essex, we add travel to the written quote.

Do you send carol singers to Chelmsford at Christmas?

Yes. Through December we sing a cappella carol services, and sets that move round offices, hotels, and residents’ events in Chelmsford and across Essex. Our Christmas page covers what we offer.
