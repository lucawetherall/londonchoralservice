Install on the iPhone

  1. With Tailscale connected, open this page's address in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. In the Shortcuts app, make a shortcut called "LCS": Tailscale → Connect, then Open URL {{ origin or "https://..ts.net" }}/. Add it to the Home Screen and open the app with it.

With the VPN off, the app shows the last Today and Money pages it saw, marked "Couldn't reach the Mac (showing the copy from …)". Actions need the VPN. A copy older than 7 days isn't shown, and signing out clears them.
