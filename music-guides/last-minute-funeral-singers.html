Booking funeral singers at short notice

By Luca Wetherall, Artistic Director & Tutor in Music, University of Oxford

Published 20 August 2026 · Updated 24 October 2026

If the funeral is days away and the music is not settled, this guide covers what we can arrange in the time available, what to have ready when you call, and what happens after you get in touch.

Can you book funeral singers at short notice? Yes. We hold diary space for short-notice funerals and confirm availability the same day you ask. Most families contact us one or two weeks before the service, and we have covered services booked within forty-eight hours of the first call. With two or three days to go, a soloist or a choir of four singing well-known hymns is the usual choice.

Short notice costs nothing extra: a soloist is £250 and a choir of four £1,150, with travel within Greater London included. Tell us the date on our funerals page or call 07356 042468.

Our singers performing Abide with Me, one of the most-requested funeral hymns. Hear more recordings.

How fast a booking can move

Nobody books a funeral a year ahead, so we hold diary space for short-notice bookings and confirm availability the same day you ask about a date. We have arranged singers at a few hours’ notice before.

The booking itself is short work. You tell us the date, the venue and what you have in mind; we send a written quote with everything included; you say yes. Our guide on how to book a choir sets out each step, and if you have more time and are comparing providers, our guide to choosing funeral singers in London covers the training and the questions to ask.

What the notice period changes

With two weeks or more, the full range is open: any ensemble from a soloist to a twelve-voice funeral choir, and repertoire beyond our core list. A new arrangement of an unusual piece needs the most lead time, because we write it, rehearse it, and send you a recording before the day.

Inside a week, the ensemble sizes are usually still open, and the core funeral repertoire needs no extra rehearsal: Abide with Me, The Lord’s My Shepherd, Pie Jesu, Ave Maria, and the other hymns and solo pieces we sing most often. Within two or three days, a soloist or a quartet singing from that repertoire is the option we usually suggest.

What to have ready

Three details settle most of a short-notice booking: the date and time of the service, the venue, and whether the music is already chosen. If it is not chosen, that need not slow anything down — our funeral music guide walks through the choices, and we will suggest a programme to fit the service you describe. If a funeral director or celebrant is arranging things, tell us who: we liaise with them directly on timings, cues, and the order of service.

What happens after you get in touch

Call 07356 042468, or send the form on our funerals page. We confirm availability the same day. If the funeral is this week, WhatsApp us first; the written quote and any recordings follow by email.

From acceptance onward, the music is off your list. We prepare the repertoire, coordinate with the venue and the funeral director, and arrive at least forty-five minutes before the service to warm up and settle the cues.

What it costs

Short notice costs the same as any other booking. A soloist is £250, a small choir of four £1,150, a quintet £1,400, a sextet £1,600 and a choir of eight £2,000; an organist or pianist added to a booking is £250. Each price covers up to two hours, including a rehearsal, with travel within Greater London and no VAT added. If the service is ten days away or fewer, we ask for a single payment rather than two instalments. Our funeral music costs guide breaks the figures down.

Sources: our funerals page, pricing page and FAQ for notice, prices and payment.

Frequently asked questions

Can you sing at a funeral this week?

Usually, yes. We hold diary space for short-notice bookings and confirm availability the same day you ask. Most funerals reach us with one or two weeks’ notice, and we have covered services booked within forty-eight hours of the first call.

How quickly will you reply to a short-notice enquiry?

Luca reads every enquiry and replies the same working day, usually within a few hours. If the funeral is this week, message us on WhatsApp first; the written quote follows by email.

Is there an extra charge for a short-notice funeral booking?

No. A short-notice booking costs the same as any other: £250 for a soloist and £1,150 for a choir of four, with travel within Greater London included and no VAT added.

How do we pay when the funeral is only days away?

If the service is ten days away or fewer, we ask for a single payment rather than two instalments. Your booking confirmation sets out the amount and the date.

What music can a choir prepare in a few days?

The hymns and solo pieces we sing most often need no extra preparation: Abide with Me, The Lord’s My Shepherd, Pie Jesu, Ave Maria, and the rest of the core funeral repertoire. A new arrangement of an unusual piece needs more lead time, because we write it, rehearse it, and send you a recording before the day.

Can our funeral director make the booking for us?

Yes. Funeral directors book us on behalf of families regularly. We liaise with them on timings, cues, and the order of service, and the invoice can go to the family or to the funeral home, whichever suits.

We’re here if you’d like a hand

If the service is close and the music is still unsettled, that is the position most families are in when they first get in touch. There is no charge for the conversation and no obligation.
