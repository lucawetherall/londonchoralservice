Wedding music guides

These guides cover the music for a wedding ceremony, from hymns and organ pieces to choral anthems and pop songs arranged for a choir. Luca Wetherall, our Artistic Director, wrote them, and each one names the pieces and who sings or plays them. When you are ready to book singers, our page on wedding choir hire has the sizes and prices, and every fee is on our pricing page.

If you are starting from nothing, read the ceremony overview first: it takes a church wedding in order, from the prelude to the recessional, and points to the guide for each moment. If you already know the shape of the service, go to the group you need below. A civil ceremony in England and Wales must not include religious music, and our wedding choir page sets out what that leaves; the humanist guide in the last group lists the secular repertoire.
