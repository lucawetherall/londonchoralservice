THE ACCOUNT'S SETTINGS AND THE OWNER'S RULES
- Settings come from the report, never from memory: section 1b's "-- snapshot" lists every enabled campaign (budget, bidding, networks, end date, schedule, locations), ad group (default bid), keyword (match type, effective bid), ad (strength, final URL, pins), sitelink, asset count and conversion action, read live this morning. Its "-- changed since <date>" lists the lines added ("+ ") and removed ("- ") since the previous saved report; a changed item shows as a "- " line then a "+ " line.
- Logged changes: section 1 ends with the changes logged in logs/ads-changes.md since the previous report (and the last three weeks' anyway), newest first, one bid applied to many keywords shown once as "(+n more alike)". Each line in 1b's "changed since" is explained when a log row dated on or after that date names the same item and the same new value (a paused keyword or ad group drops out of the snapshot, so it shows only as a "- " line). An explained line is the owner's own change, not a FLAG (say "changed <date>, as logged" only if it matters to this week's numbers). A line no log row explains is a FLAG. A change in an ad's strength alone (PENDING → AVERAGE, say) is Google's rating, not a setting: report it under ADS, never as a FLAG. "no earlier snapshot (first run)" means there is nothing to compare: say so once, no FLAG.
- Budget caps: every campaign's daily budget in 1b is at most £5, except the Christmas campaign 24295921372, which may be up to £8 from 28 Sep to 13 Dec 2026 (scripts/ads/budget_cap.py). A campaign whose budget is over its cap is always a FLAG, logged or not.
- Every campaign carries the negatives singer, soloist, solo and vocalist; the negative lists grow most weeks and their size is not a finding.
- Conversion actions: "Submit lead form" and "WhatsApp or email click" primary; "Call click" and "Booked job" secondary; "Contact" and "Contact (1)" are legacy secondary actions kept for history (not a finding). Paused or removed keywords and ads don't matter unless something enabled depends on them. Section 2's matched keywords are the week's history: the singer keywords paused on 26 Sep still show there, so a matched keyword is not a finding unless the term cost money after that date. Section 1's "since 26 Sep" and the Christmas value check's "since 28 Sep" cover different days; that difference is expected. Section 2 shows only the terms Google reports; low-volume terms stay hidden, so say what share of the week's spend it covers.
- Targeting: choir bookings (weddings, funerals) and "carol singers" (plural) bookings of four or more singers only. Never propose singer, soloist or vocalist keywords, or broadening wedding/funeral with singer terms: low choir volume and underspend are expected. Keep the London Funeral Singers brand keywords.
- Never recommend Google's search partners, display expansion, broad match, Maximise conversions or Maximise clicks, or singer keywords: one line saying so. Judge its other recommendations on their merits.

STEPS
1. Run the shell command once. Everything below comes from its output unless it says otherwise. A section that ends "SECTION <id> FAILED: <type>" wasn't read this week: say "<what it covers>: not in the report (<type>)" in the matching RESULT line and carry on with the rest; never guess its numbers.
2. Performance (section 1) and ads (section 4): per campaign, the week's and since-26-Sep spend, clicks, CPC, conversions and impression share (budget-lost and rank-lost). Flag a campaign averaging over its budget, any ad not APPROVED, any final URL on http://, any enabled ad rated POOR (with Google's "to improve" advice and a proposed replacement headline or description that keeps the pins, as 1b lists them), any campaign over its budget cap, and every line in 1b's "changed since" that no logged change at the end of section 1 explains.
   - Ad quality: for each enabled ad, copy section 4's strength and "headlines=n descriptions=n"; for each campaign, its "sitelinks=n". Section 4's clicks30 shows which ads actually serve: an ad group whose ads have clicks30=0 for a whole month is low-volume, so say so once and never propose changes to its ads or final URL on the strength of message match alone. Flag a campaign with fewer than 4 enabled sitelinks. Write no replacement copy for an AVERAGE ad: the monthly growth review proposes one headline set a month for the campaign with the most clicks, under the copy skills. Only a POOR ad gets a proposal here (the rule above).
   - Lost to rank: when a campaign loses more than 40% of its impressions to rank (the week's "lost to rank"), any proposal for it is a keyword bid or an ad relevance fix, never a budget rise. Name its keywords with the most impressions (summed from section 2's matched keywords) and, for each, the bid or relevance change you would make, starting from its current bid in 1b.
3. Search terms (section 2): classify every term as HIRING (booking a choir or carol singers, including the London Funeral Singers brand), UNCLEAR, or NOT A BUYER (concerts or services to attend, lyrics, songs, jobs, objects, Dickens, solo singers or soloist acts, music research, other genres such as sangeet, mariachi, brass or jazz bands, singing waiters). Give clicks and cost per class, and the matched keyword for each NOT A BUYER term that cost money.
4. Negatives: section 2 marks each term an existing negative now blocks, "[now blocked by negative '<text>' (<match>)]", and gives "negatives in force" per campaign. A blocked term that still cost money this week was clicked before that negative went on; say so. For each NOT A BUYER term with no such mark, propose one negative (text, match type, campaign) that blocks it and blocks none of this week's HIRING terms. Google's rules: a BROAD negative blocks a search holding all its words in any order, a PHRASE negative the words together in order, EXACT the whole search; none of them catch plurals ("singer" doesn't block "singers"). If one keyword keeps pulling non-buyers, propose pausing it.
5. Tracking (sections 3, 5, 5c, 8):

Copy 5c's enquiry events in one TRACKING line; every send or tap there should show up as an enquiry in the bookings agent's list, which the dispatcher compares.
   - Conversions per action: "Submit lead form" and "WhatsApp or email click" are primary; "Call click" and "Booked job" secondary. Give the last day each was seen (the dispatcher compares that date with the week's enquiries; you don't).
   - Primary-conversion sanity: section 3 prints each action's role and status and marks "!CHECK" when an action other than "Submit lead form" and "WhatsApp or email click" is primary, when either of those two is not primary or not ENABLED, or when either is missing. Each "!CHECK" line is a FLAG (unless a logged change explains it), with a proposed change setting it back: "conversion action <name> → primary_for_goal: true → false" (or the reverse), or "→ status: <x> → ENABLED". "Contact" and "Contact (1)" as secondary are not a finding.
   - GA4: generate_lead, contact_click, contact_message and form_error with occasion, lead_source, method and error_type. If form_error outnumbers generate_lead, give the error_type breakdown.
   - Wiring: all three pages "all tags present", auto-tagging on, GA4 key events include generate_lead and contact_message, the GA4 ↔ Google Ads link present. Flag anything else.
   - Alarm, from 5 Oct 2026: no generate_lead AND no contact_click for the whole week while Paid Search or Organic sessions are above zero → "ALARM:" plus what section 8 shows. If section 5 says !THRESHOLDED, judge from section 3's Ads conversions instead; if that is two weeks running, suggest switching GA4's Reporting identity to Device-based (Admin → Data display → Reporting identity).

6. Ad clicks by day: copy section 2's "ad clicks by day" lines as "<date> <campaign> <clicks>", for reference only: the report grades every enquiry itself (section 9b, the bookings agent's ENQUIRIES THIS WEEK).

An enquiry from a campaign means an A or B enquiry for it; C is shown and never counts, for or against.
   - Spend guard (owner decision 26 Oct 2026: only an A or B enquiry holds a campaign; Ads conversions are shown for information and no longer hold it, since 134 ad clicks this season brought no enquiry while two click-conversions kept the funeral campaign open): copy each "spend guard" line; each gives the campaign's Ads conversions and its A/B/C enquiries in the 28 days. "held" (an A or B enquiry came from it), "deferred" (a campaign inside its dated budget exception, such as Christmas at £8 to 13 Dec 2026, judged by the value check instead) and "too few to judge" are reported only.

Keep £8 while it earns its place. Say whether the campaign uses the extra (averaging over £5/day or losing impression share to budget); if not, the bids or the searches are the limit.
   - After 20 Dec 2026: replace the value check with a Christmas season summary (spend, clicks, conversions) and propose pausing the campaign (never deleting), once.
